About me

My research sits between accounting and information economics. Prediction markets now list contracts on individual public firms, and their prices are a continuously updated, freely visible probability on events that bear on valuation. I ask what that does to a firm's information environment: whether it lowers the cost of equity, and whether the analysts who cover the firm take it up, ignore it, or are displaced by it.

Before returning to research I spent nine years in finance, as a financial analyst and then a finance manager at technology companies in Tehran.

I work with large survey and panel data, Bayesian inference, functional data analysis and text as data. My applied statistics work with Moinak Bhaduri at Bentley appears in Chance and Nightingale.

Background

I hold an MS in Business Analytics from Bentley University, where I am a data analytics research assistant, and a BS in Accounting from Shahid Beheshti University in Tehran.
