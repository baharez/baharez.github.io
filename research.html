Do Prediction Markets Lower the Cost of Equity?In progress

Bahareh Zahirodini

Asks whether firm-level prediction markets reduce a firm's implied cost of equity by lowering the cost of processing information about it. Polymarket contracts are tagged to firms and linked to Compustat, and the implied cost of equity is estimated with the GLS, Ohlson-Juettner, PEG and modified PEG measures over 2018 to 2026. The design tests whether the effect grows with market liquidity and with how much uncertainty surrounded the firm to begin with.

Marking Contemporary America's Attitudinal Stance Towards Modern Businesses: a Bayesian Attempt to Detect Deviation Patterns Based on Demographics and PoliticsPublished

Bahareh Zahirodini, Moinak Bhaduri

Chance, volume 38.2, 2025. American Statistical Association

Analysed over 5,000 Gallup-Bentley survey responses to see how views on business ethics shift across demographic and political lines. Bayesian inference and latent space modelling estimate the probability that large segments of the population support particular ethical practices, uncovering gaps between what people say they value and what they believe others do.
